    Achievements

    Student Achievements

    Students at Alliance University have achieved recognition in academics, sports, innovation, entrepreneurship, and technical competitions. Students have participated in national and international competitions, hackathons, gameathons, research forums, and other co-curricular activities. In the field of immersive technology, students have earned 150+ Unity certifications, completed 30+ industry-oriented internships, and received major recognitions, including First Prize at the GameForge 1.0 National Gameathon and First Place in the Quantum X Game Genesis Competition..

    Faculty Achievements

    Alliance University has a highly qualified and accomplished faculty with strong academic and research backgrounds. Faculty members have received national and international recognition for their research, publications, awards, patents, and academic contributions. Several professors are recognised among the World’s Top 2% Scientists, while others have secured research grants and contributed to reputed international journals and conferences. The university also supports faculty innovation through research centres, Centres of Excellence, and funded research projects..
